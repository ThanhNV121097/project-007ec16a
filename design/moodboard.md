# Hairline — how this site is built

This is the **built** form of the template *Hairline*: white, hairline rules
and big quiet type, the goods on pale grey tiles, and nothing else coloured.
It was built once and approved, and a project that picks the template starts
from it. The picture it was built to is `design/template.jpg`.

There is no direction to choose here. What is left to decide is how one
business sits in it:

- the **home page** opens on ONE thing at full size — the business's best
  seller, or the thing it wants seen first — not on a photograph of a scene;
- the **catalogue** is the tiles, four across, each with its caption row;
- a **record's page** is the template's own picture: the name large at the
  left, the tile at the right, and everything a buyer asks behind three
  disclosure rows;
- colour arrives only inside the photographs. The page has none of its own.

What the template has that this system cannot make: the goods photographed
one to a frame on a flat grey ground. Use the owner's own where they have
them and a stock photograph of the thing on a plain light backdrop where they do not — never
a lifestyle shot in a tile.

## COMMITMENTS

- A 1px hairline rule runs the full content width under the header and between every band of every page; nothing else divides the page.
- Every product picture sits alone on a square-cornered pale grey tile, with no border, no shadow and no label inside it.
- Under every tile, one caption row: the name in letterspaced capitals at the left, the price at the right.
- The home page's first screen is one product — its name large over two lines at the left, its tile filling the right half — and no photograph across the top.
- A record's page carries three disclosure rows (details, care, delivery) each a label and a + with a hairline between.
- The one call to action is an outlined pill with small letterspaced capitals and an arrow; nothing on the page is filled with colour.
