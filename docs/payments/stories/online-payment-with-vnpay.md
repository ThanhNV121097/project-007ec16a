# Story — Online payment with VNPay

Module: `payments` · Plan item 8 · Requirements: PAY-001, PAY-002, PAY-003, PAY-004, PAY-005 (`docs/payments/SRS.md`); the payment half of ORD-006 (items 4–6, AC-7, AC-8, AC-10, AC-11) and ORD-007 item 9 (`docs/orders/SRS.md`)
Design system: `design/design-system.md` (§2.1.5 outline pill, §2.1.6 filled pill, §2.1.28 icon) · Architecture: `docs/architecture/overview.md`, `services.md` (§3.5 payment options, §3.11 `GET /v1/orders/{id}`, §3.13 `POST /v1/orders/{id}/payment`, §3.17 `GET /v1/payments/vnpay/ipn`, §2.8 the order's public view), `erd.md` (§3.12 `orders`, §3.16 `payment_attempts`, §3.17 `payment_notices`)

> Owner decision that shapes this story: the owner chose **online payment only**. VNPay is the one way to pay, there is no cash on delivery, and no order can be completed without it. So the "30-minute rule" applies to every unpaid order placed through checkout, and checkout offers VNPay as its only choice. Two things stay as the SRS has them: the 14 sample orders that came with the first design (`DOI-24804` to `DOI-24817`) are never cancelled by the rule, so the dashboard figures hold (PAY-003 item 4, open question 7); and an unpaid order the dealer has already shipped or delivered is left alone (PAY-003 item 1).

## 1. User story

As a **customer** (a guest holding the order's token in this browser — no account needed), I want to pay for my order on VNPay's page and then be told plainly whether the payment worked, so that the pairs set aside for me are mine, I never pay twice, and if I close the tab I can still pay later from my order.

Other actors this story touches:

- **VNPay** (the payment gateway) — calls the shop's notice address from its own servers; the signature is the only thing that proves who is calling.
- **Owner** — supplies `VNPAY_TMN_CODE` and `VNPAY_HASH_SECRET` (sandbox first) in the console and registers the notice address `/api/v1/payments/vnpay/ipn` in VNPay's merchant portal. Both are already requested (`request_secret` ×2, `request_setup` ×1).
- **Dealer** (owner or staff) — sees an order as paid, with VNPay's transaction number, only because VNPay said so; refunds in VNPay's own portal a payment that arrived after the order was given up. The dealer never marks an online order paid.
- **Stranger** — anyone who is not VNPay, calling the notice address, learns nothing and changes nothing; anyone without an order's token learns nothing about it from the result page.

## 2. What exists today, and where this story plugs in

"Checkout and placing an order" (plan item 5) shipped the **payment seam**: one provider interface that each way to pay plugs into, `GET /v1/payment-options` (VNPay alone, `is_available` true until a provider checks its keys), `POST /v1/orders` answering `payment_url: ""` while no provider exists, the `order_tokens` table and the browser's store of placed orders in `src/lib/orders.ts`. "Order page and Pay now" (plan item 6) shipped `POST /v1/orders/{id}/payment`, which answers 409 "Online payment is not available yet." for every payable order until a provider is registered, and the Order page's "Pay now" button, which already goes to the address in a `200 {"payment_url"}` answer and treats every other outcome as a failure.

This story **registers the `vnpay` provider behind that seam**. Nothing the browser draws at checkout or on the order page changes shape: the pages already branch on an empty or non-empty `payment_url`, on `is_available` and on the 409/503 answers. What is new for a visitor is the Payment result page.

## 3. In scope

**Backend (Go, PostgreSQL)**

- The `vnpay` provider, registered in the payment seam. Its `Available()` is the key check: both `VNPAY_TMN_CODE` and `VNPAY_HASH_SECRET` set. Its `PaymentURL` builds VNPay's signed payment address locally (no call to VNPay): the terminal code, the amount **read from the order in the database** in VNPay's unit, VND, a description naming the order number, a reference different for every attempt, the shop's `/payment/return` as the return address, the order's 30-minute deadline as the moment the payment page stops accepting payment, signed with HMAC-SHA512 over the sorted parameters (`services.md` §3.13). Each call stores one payment attempt under the order's row lock.
- With the provider registered: `POST /v1/orders` answers `payment_url` with the address (and 503 `payment_unavailable`, creating nothing, when a key is missing); `POST /v1/orders/{id}/payment` answers `200 {"payment_url"}` for an unpaid, not cancelled VNPay order (and 503 when a key is missing), keeping its existing 404 and 409 answers; `GET /v1/payment-options` reports `vnpay` with `is_available` false while a key is missing.
- `GET /v1/payments/vnpay/ipn` (`services.md` §3.17): VNPay's notice. Checks the signature, the reference, the amount and that the order is not already paid, in that order; answers HTTP 200 with VNPay's own `{"RspCode","Message"}` body; marks the order paid exactly once — and **is the only thing that does**. A confirmed order still at "placed" becomes "confirmed". A verified notice for an order already cancelled records the payment and sets "paid" while the status stays "cancelled".
- The notice's data kept: for each notice, the attempt, VNPay's transaction number, bank, amount, response code, whether it succeeded, and the payment time (`payment_notices`). No card number is received or kept.
- The 30-minute expiry: an in-process check at least once a minute cancels every unpaid order whose status is placed, confirmed or packed and which is 30 minutes old, puts its pairs back on the shelf once, and brings the shoe's sold-out list, last-pairs list and "pairs in stock" up to date. It skips the 14 sample orders, paid orders, cancelled orders and shipped or delivered orders.
- The two new tables `payment_attempts` and `payment_notices` and the partial index for the expiry scan (`erd.md` §3.16, §3.17, `idx_orders_unpaid_open`), each in its own migration.
- Settings: `VNPAY_TMN_CODE`, `VNPAY_HASH_SECRET` (secrets, key names only in `.env.example`), and the optional `VNPAY_PAYMENT_URL` (defaults to VNPay's sandbox page; going live changes this value, not the code) and `PUBLIC_BASE_URL` (override for the return address when the proxy's forwarded headers are not enough).

**Frontend (Vite + React + Tailwind tokens)**

- A new page `src/pages/PaymentReturn.tsx` (and parts under `src/components/` when it nears 300 lines) at the route `/payment/return`, with its one `<Route>` line and import in `src/App.tsx` (purely additive). It is drawn in the page shell (header and footer) from the site's own parts: display heading, soft line, outline and filled pills.
- A client file `src/lib/online-payment-with-vnpay.ts` on `src/lib/api.ts`: reads the order's state with the token this browser holds (through the token store's own reader in `src/lib/orders.ts` — no second store, no new storage key) and asks `POST /v1/orders/{id}/payment` for "Try again". It never marks anything paid and never decides a result from the address alone.
- The page reads two things from VNPay's return address: the reference (`vnp_TxnRef`, `<order number>-<n>`, used only to choose which stored token to ask with) and the response code (`vnp_ResponseCode`, used only to choose between "confirming" and "not completed" for an order the shop still shows unpaid).

Tokens only (`bg-ground`, `text-ink`, `border-line`, `text-danger` for the one failure line …), no arbitrary colours, no CSS modules, every file under 300 lines. One h1 per page; the state line is a `role="status"` region so a change from "confirming" to "paid" is announced.

Where each state wins when several things are true: **cancelled** (the order's status) beats **paid**, which beats **confirming** (unpaid, the address says success), which beats **not completed** (unpaid, the address says failure, cancelled by the customer, or carries no response code). A cancelled order whose payment arrived late is therefore drawn as cancelled; the dealer sees it as paid and refunds it (PAY-002 item 9).

**Copy and content keys.** Every word above is a content key the owner can edit in the Studio, like the rest of the site: the UI PR lists each key under `pages.payment_return.*` and TL says where it is added (precedent: the Order page's five keys were added in its BE PR, new keys only). `src/content.json` itself is frozen for stories.

**Hooks for tests.** `data-testid="payment-state"` on the h1, with `data-state` one of `paid`, `confirming`, `timed-out`, `not-completed`, `cancelled`, `no-match`, `check-failed`; `payment-line` (the state line), `payment-view-order`, `payment-try-again`, `payment-track`, `payment-keep-shopping` — so no case depends on translated words alone.

## 6. Acceptance criteria

Each criterion names the SRS requirement and criterion it narrows (`SC-n [REQ AC-m]`). Test writes at least one case per `SC`; TL's reviews count this list. "Shop" means the running API; "the address" is the VNPay payment address; amounts are VND with `vi-VN` formatting on the shop's pages; **VNPay's own unit is the amount × 100** (`vnp_Amount=314000000` is 3.140.000 ₫).

How the cases are driven (so a case is not reported green for something it could not observe):

- VNPay's servers cannot reach a deployment on a `.localhost` address, so wherever VNPay would call the notice address the case sends the **same request VNPay sends**, signed with the sandbox secret by the same algorithm (HMAC-SHA512 over the sorted `vnp_` parameters). A case that needs the real VNPay sandbox page is marked as such and is only fully observed with a public address.
- The keys are a precondition of the run. Without them every criterion that needs a built address or a verified notice is **UNVERIFIED** (the instrument it lacked: `VNPAY_TMN_CODE` and `VNPAY_HASH_SECRET`), and only the "keys missing" criteria (SC-8, SC-9) are observed.
- The dealer's moves and times are set directly in the test database where the dealer's screens do not exist yet: an order's status, its placed time (31 minutes old, 2 hours old), `is_sample`, and "the dealer cancelled it at minute 5".

### A. The payment address, "Pay now", and when the keys are missing (PAY-001, PAY-005)

- **SC-1 [PAY-001 AC-1, AC-2; item 3]** (BE): For an unpaid VNPay order whose stored total is 3.140.000 ₫, `POST /v1/orders/<id>/payment` with the order's token answers 200 `{"payment_url": …}` and the address carries: the shop's terminal code (`vnp_TmnCode`), `vnp_Amount=314000000`, `vnp_CurrCode=VND`, a `vnp_TxnRef` holding the order number (`DOI-24818-1`), and a `vnp_OrderInfo` naming the order number; its host and path are `VNPAY_PAYMENT_URL`'s. An amount sent by the browser — a total of 1.000 ₫ in the placing request, `?amount=1000` on the call — changes nothing: the address still carries the stored total.
- **SC-2 [PAY-001 AC-3, item 5]** (BE): Asking for the address twice for one order gives two different references (`DOI-24818-1`, `DOI-24818-2`), each at most 100 characters, with two attempts stored; two asks at the same moment never get the same reference; a correctly signed success notice for the **first** reference still finds the order and pays it.
- **SC-3 [PAY-001 AC-4, AC-5, item 4]** (BE): Recomputing HMAC-SHA512, with the shop's hash secret, over every `vnp_` parameter of the address except `vnp_SecureHash` (sorted by name, values encoded as VNPay requires) gives exactly `vnp_SecureHash`; with another secret it does not. `vnp_ReturnUrl` is the shop's `/payment/return` — on `PUBLIC_BASE_URL` when that is set, else on the scheme and host the request arrived with.
- **SC-4 [PAY-001 AC-6, boundary row; PAY-003 item 6]** (BE): For an order placed 10 minutes ago the address's `vnp_ExpireDate` is 20 minutes from now (the order's placed time + 30 minutes, `yyyyMMddHHmmss`, Vietnam time); for an order placed 29 minutes 30 seconds ago an address is still built, expiring at the same deadline; for an unpaid, not cancelled order whose 30 minutes have passed, `POST /v1/orders/<id>/payment` answers 409 `conflict` and no attempt is stored.
- **SC-5 [PAY-001 AC-9; ORD-006 AC-7; ORD-009 AC-3]** (BE + UI): With the keys set, a pair chosen and checkout completed with VNPay: `POST /v1/orders` answers 201 with a non-empty `payment_url`; the bag is emptied; the browser goes to that address (navigation observed at VNPay's host); and its `vnp_Amount` equals the Total shown on the checkout page × 100, which equals the order's stored total.
- **SC-6 [PAY-001 items 1, 7; ORD-007 AC-14, AC-17, AC-18, AC-19, AC-20; ORD-006 item 4]** (BE + UI): With the keys set, "Pay now" on an unpaid, not cancelled VNPay order answers 200 with a fresh address (a new attempt, a new reference) and the Order page's "Pay now" — disabled while it waits — sends the browser there; the story edits no line of that page. For a paid order, a cancelled order and a sample order whose way to pay is not VNPay the answer is still 409 `conflict` with the three different sentences of the Order page story; with no token, another order's token or an id that does not exist it is still the one 404 body. The answer "Online payment is not available yet." is never given for a VNPay order once the keys are set.
- **SC-7 [ORD-006 AC-8, item 5; PAY-001 item 1]** (BE + UI): When the address cannot be built after the order was saved (the attempt cannot be stored), the order still exists — unpaid, status "placed", its pairs taken — the answer's `payment_url` is `""`, the browser goes to `/orders/<id>?placed=1`, and the Order page shows "Pay now". (Backend test with a store that fails; browser check with the answer stubbed.)
- **SC-8 [PAY-001 AC-8, AC-10, item 6; PAY-005 AC-2; ORD-006 AC-11]** (BE): With `VNPAY_TMN_CODE` or `VNPAY_HASH_SECRET` unset: the server still starts and `/healthz` answers 200; `GET /v1/payment-options` lists `vnpay` with `is_available: false`; `POST /v1/orders` with a valid body answers 503 `payment_unavailable` after the field checks and before any write — no order exists, the next order number is unchanged and no pair left the shelf; "Pay now" for an unpaid VNPay order with its token answers 503 `payment_unavailable` (access is checked first, then state, then keys: a stranger still gets 404 and a paid order still 409); the notice address answers `97` to everything.
- **SC-9 [PAY-001 AC-7; ORD-001 AC-14; ORD-006 AC-10, item 6]** (UI): With a key missing, `/checkout` with a pair in the bag shows the VNPay row, under it "Online payment is not available right now. Please try again later." in alert style, and "Place order" disabled (pressing it sends nothing). When the page was loaded with the keys set and the shop then answers 503 `payment_unavailable` to placing, the same line is shown and the bag and every typed detail are kept.
- **SC-10 [PAY-005 AC-1; ORD-006 AC-1, AC-2, AC-5; owner decision]** (BE + UI): With both keys set, `GET /v1/payment-options` lists `vnpay` alone with `is_available: true`; checkout step 03 shows one row, "VNPay — ATM, Visa, Mastercard", selected, and "Place order" is enabled; no row for cash on delivery, bank transfer or MoMo; a placing request with `payment` set to `cod`, `bank` or `momo` is refused 422 naming `payment` and creates no order — no order can be completed without VNPay.

### B. VNPay's notice is the only thing that marks an order paid (PAY-002)

- **SC-11 [PAY-002 AC-1, AC-2, items 1–4; Privacy NFR]** (BE): For an unpaid VNPay order of 3.140.000 ₫ at status "placed" with attempt reference R, `GET /v1/payments/vnpay/ipn` with no session and no token, carrying a correctly signed success notice (`vnp_TxnRef=R`, `vnp_Amount=314000000`, `vnp_ResponseCode=00`, `vnp_TransactionStatus=00`, our terminal code, a transaction number, a bank code and a pay time) answers HTTP 200 `{"RspCode":"00","Message":"Confirm Success"}`; the order is paid and its status is now "confirmed"; the transaction number, bank, amount (3.140.000), response code and pay time are kept with the order (observed in the database, and in the dealer's read of the order where it exists). An order already at "packed" is marked paid and stays "packed". Nothing kept holds a card number.
- **SC-12 [PAY-002 AC-3, AC-4, item 6]** (BE): The same notice a second time answers `{"RspCode":"02","Message":"Order already confirmed"}` and changes nothing. Two copies — and, in a second run, twenty — sent at the same moment: exactly one answers `00`, every other `02`; the order is paid once; exactly one successful notice is kept.
- **SC-13 [PAY-002 AC-5, AC-10, item 7]** (BE): A notice whose signature is altered by one character, one signed with another secret, one with no `vnp_SecureHash`, one for a terminal code that is not the shop's, and a request with no query at all each answer 200 with `RspCode` `97` and change nothing: the order stays unpaid and nothing is kept.
- **SC-14 [PAY-002 AC-6, AC-7, item 2]** (BE): A correctly signed notice for the wrong amount (`vnp_Amount=100000` for a 3.140.000 ₫ order, or a non-numeric amount) answers `04`; a correctly signed notice whose reference matches no order answers `01`; neither changes anything. The checks run signature, then reference, then amount, then "already paid": a notice that is wrong in two ways gets the first code.
- **SC-15 [PAY-002 AC-8, item 5]** (BE): A correctly signed notice for the right amount whose response code says the customer cancelled (`vnp_ResponseCode=24`), or whose transaction status is not `00`, answers `00`; the order stays unpaid with its status unchanged and the failed notice is kept; a correctly signed success notice for the same order's next attempt afterwards marks it paid and answers `00`.
- **SC-16 [PAY-002 AC-11, item 9; PAY-003 conflict row]** (BE): A correctly signed success notice for an order already cancelled answers `00`; the payment is kept, "paid" is true and the status is still "cancelled"; the order's pairs are not taken off the shelf again and not returned again.
- **SC-17 [PAY-002 AC-12, item 3; failure row]** (BE): Every answer from the notice address — `00`, `01`, `02`, `04`, `97` — is HTTP 200 with a JSON body of exactly `RspCode` and `Message`, never the project's error envelope, and a notice carrying an extra `vnp_` field is not refused with 400. When the database fails while a signed success notice is processed (a store that fails, in a backend test) the answer is `99` "Unknown error", nothing is half-written (the order is unpaid and no notice is kept), and the same notice sent again afterwards is processed normally.
- **SC-18 [PAY-002 AC-9, item 8; PAY-004 AC-9]** (BE + UI): The browser never marks anything paid: `/payment/return?vnp_TxnRef=…&vnp_ResponseCode=00&vnp_SecureHash=…` opened for an unpaid order in the browser that holds its token, and `POST /v1/orders/<id>/payment`, leave the order unpaid, status unchanged, when it is read 60 seconds later.
- **SC-19 [NFR Integrity, Performance]** (BE): In 100 of 100 attempts a notice with a bad signature, an unknown reference or a wrong amount changes no order; in 100 of 100 runs two identical notices at the same moment mark the order paid once; and over 100 sequential signed notices the notice address answers within 2 s at p95.

### C. An unpaid order is given up after 30 minutes (PAY-003)

- **SC-20 [PAY-003 AC-1, AC-2, items 1–3]** (BE): A VNPay order placed through checkout 31 minutes ago, unpaid, status "placed", holding 2 pairs of size `38`: once the check has run (it runs at least once a minute, so the order is cancelled within 31 minutes of being placed; an order exactly 30 minutes old is cancelled by the next run) its status is "cancelled", size `38` has 2 more pairs, and the shoe's sold-out list, last-pairs list and "pairs in stock" agree with the counts. An order of two shoes returns both.
- **SC-21 [PAY-003 AC-3, AC-4, AC-6, AC-10, item 4]** (BE): The check leaves unchanged: an unpaid order placed 29 minutes ago; a paid order placed 2 hours ago; an unpaid order placed 2 hours ago that the dealer moved to "shipped" (and the same for "delivered") — status stays, still unpaid, its pairs are not returned; and an order the dealer cancelled at minute 5, at minute 31 — its pairs are not returned a second time (the counts are as they were).
- **SC-22 [PAY-003 AC-5]** (BE): An unpaid order placed through checkout 2 hours ago that the dealer moved to "confirmed" — and, in a second case, to "packed" — is cancelled by the check and its pairs return to the shelf.
- **SC-23 [PAY-003 AC-8, conflict row; PAY-002 item 9]** (BE): Two checks run at the same moment over one order that is due: it is cancelled once and its pairs return once (the count rises by the order's quantity, not twice). A success notice arriving as the check runs: exactly one wins — the order is paid and left alone with its pairs still taken, or it is cancelled once with its pairs returned once and then carries "paid" true with status "cancelled" (SC-16); in neither outcome are pairs returned twice.
- **SC-24 [PAY-003 AC-9, item 4; SRS open question 7]** (BE): The 14 sample orders `DOI-24804` to `DOI-24817` — those unpaid, in status placed, confirmed or packed, and placed more than 30 minutes ago — are unchanged after the check, and so are the pairs on the shelf for them; an order placed through checkout is never marked as a sample.
- **SC-25 [PAY-003 AC-7, item 5; PAY-001 item 7; ORD-007 AC-16]** (BE + UI): For a VNPay order cancelled by the check, `/orders/<id>` with its token shows the existing cancelled view ("This order was cancelled.") and no "Pay now", and `POST /v1/orders/<id>/payment` answers 409 saying it was cancelled.
- **SC-26 [PAY-004 item 7, AC-13; PAY-001 item 1]** (UI + BE): A customer who placed a VNPay order and closed VNPay's page without paying, opening `/orders/<id>` 10 minutes later, sees "Pay now"; pressing it goes to a fresh VNPay address (a new reference). Once the 30 minutes have passed and the check has run, the same page shows the cancelled view and no "Pay now".

### D. The Payment result page (PAY-004)

The cases open `/payment/return` the way VNPay's return does — `vnp_TxnRef=<order number>-<n>` and `vnp_ResponseCode=<code>` in the address — in a browser whose store of placed orders holds (or does not hold) the order's token.

- **SC-27 [PAY-004 AC-1, items 1–3]** (UI): For an order marked paid by VNPay's notice, `/payment/return?vnp_TxnRef=DOI-24818-1&vnp_ResponseCode=00` shows the h1 "Payment received." (`data-state="paid"`), the line "Your order DOI-24818 is paid. We will pack it next." and a "View your order" link to `/orders/doi-24818`; there is no "Try again". The paid state is drawn whatever response code the address carries.
- **SC-28 [PAY-004 AC-2, AC-3, item 2; Accessibility NFR]** (UI): With the address saying success and the order still unpaid (no notice yet) the page shows the h1 "We are confirming your payment…", the line "This usually takes a few seconds. Please do not pay again.", a "View your order" link and **no** "Try again". It asks the shop again every 3 seconds; when the notice arrives after 6 seconds the page switches to "Payment received." with no reload, and the state line is a `role="status"` region so the change is announced.
- **SC-29 [PAY-004 AC-4, AC-9, item 2]** (UI): The same, with no notice for 30 seconds: the h1 becomes "We have not heard from VNPay yet.", the line "If money left your account it will show on your order once VNPay confirms it. Please do not pay again.", a "View your order" link, and still no "Try again"; the page stops asking. The order read 60 seconds after opening the page is still unpaid.
- **SC-30 [PAY-004 AC-5, AC-6, item 3]** (UI + BE): With the address saying the customer cancelled (`vnp_ResponseCode=24`, or any code other than `00`, or no code) and the order unpaid, the page shows the h1 "Payment was not completed.", the line "You can try again, or place a new order.", a "View your order" link and a "Try again" button. Pressing "Try again" (disabled while it waits) asks the shop for a new address — a new reference — and sends the browser to it. ## 8. Dependencies

- **Plan item 5, "Checkout and placing an order"** (status failed, BE PR #50 open): the `orders` package with the payment-provider interface and `Options{Providers}`, `GET /v1/payment-options`, `POST /v1/orders`, the `order_tokens` and `counters` tables, per-size stock (`product_sizes`, `product_stock`), `src/lib/orders.ts` and its token store. **Must be merged first**; there is nothing to plug the provider into before it.
- **Plan item 6, "Order page and Pay now"** (BE not started, UI PR #41 open): `POST /v1/orders/{id}/payment` with its one access check and its 404/409 answers, and the Order page's "Pay now". This story changes the route's last answer from 409 "not available yet" to 200 or 503. **Must be merged first.**
- **Plan item 3, "Site data from the database"** (merged): the `orders`, `order_items` tables and the `is_sample` flag on the 14 seeded orders, the shoes' stock lists.
- **Plan item 9, "Dealer sign-in and protected admin area"** (merged): the dealer session, for the criteria that read an order as the dealer.
- **Soft:** plan item 10, "Manage shoes, lines, categories, reviews and orders" owns the dealer's moves (confirm, pack, ship, cancel). This story does not wait for it: the statuses its criteria need are set directly in the test database, and the dealer's cancel path returns pairs through the same single-cancel rule this story's expiry uses (`UPDATE … WHERE status IN ('placed','confirmed','packed')`, pairs returned only if a row changed) so the two never return a pair twice.
- **The owner** supplies `VNPAY_TMN_CODE` and `VNPAY_HASH_SECRET` (sandbox first, from a sandbox merchant at sandbox.vnpayment.vn) and registers `/api/v1/payments/vnpay/ipn` in VNPay's merchant portal; both are asked for in the console. Until the keys are in, the shop **cannot complete any order**: checkout shows "Online payment is not available right now. Please try again later." and placing answers 503. Until the notice address is registered on a publicly reachable deployment, VNPay cannot confirm a payment, so a real sandbox payment stays "confirming" on the result page.

## 9. Size

39 acceptance criteria (SC-1 to SC-39) in one story: the address, the notice and the expiry share the attempt and notice tables and the order's row lock, and the result page is where their outcomes become visible, so splitting them would make each half unverifiable alone. If a verification round shows the notice or the expiry needs its own fixes, the expiry (SC-20 to SC-26) is the coherent part to move into its own item.
