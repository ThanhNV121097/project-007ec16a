# Story — Customer accounts: sign up, sign in, my orders

Module: `accounts` · Plan item 15 · Requirements narrowed: ACC-001, ACC-002, ACC-003, ACC-004, ACC-005, ACC-006, ACC-007, ACC-008 (`docs/accounts/SRS.md`)
Design: sign-up, sign-in and My account are **not drawn** in the approved design; they are built from the site's own parts (`design/design-system.md` §2.1.3 header, §2.1.4 menu, §2.1.5 outline pill, §2.1.6 filled pill, §2.1.16 text field and label). Constraints: `docs/architecture/overview.md`; contracts `docs/architecture/services.md` §3.18–3.21 (accounts), §3.7–3.11 (sign-in, `me`, placing and reading an order), §2.7 (limits); tables `docs/architecture/erd.md` (`users`, `orders.account_id`).

## 1. User story

As a **customer** — someone who buys from Đôi more than once — I want to make an account with my name, email, phone and a password, sign in, keep my delivery details and see my own orders, so that I do not retype my details at every checkout and can find an order without keeping its number.

Other actors this story touches:

- **Guest (a visitor with no session).** Unchanged: buys with no account, tracks an order by number and phone. Nothing in this story may ask a guest to sign in.
- **Another customer.** Sees nothing of the first customer's orders or details: every such read answers 404 like a record that does not exist.
- **Shop team (admin, staff).** Not served by these screens. A team member who signs in on `/account/sign-in` is taken to `/admin`; the header's entry shows their name and goes to `/admin`.

## 2. What exists today

- `src/lib/session.ts` is the design's mock (a `localStorage` key). "Dealer sign-in and protected admin area" (plan item 9) rewrites it over `/v1/auth/sign-in`, `/v1/auth/sign-out` and `/v1/auth/me`, adds the `users` and `sessions` tables, the `session` cookie, `auth.Resolve` / `auth.Require` and the `internal/ratelimit` helper. This story **uses** all of that and changes none of its behaviour.
- `SiteHeader.tsx` has the wordmark, five links, search and bag buttons and a menu below 1024px; no "Account" entry. `SiteFooter` has "Dealer sign-in".
- `pages/Checkout.tsx` has the four contact fields and a note, empty at the start. `pages/Order.tsx` shows an order read through the order token (plan items 5 and 6).
- No `/account/*` route, no `internal/account` package, no `orders.account_id` column exist. `code/backend/internal/` holds only `config`, `db`, `httpx`, `server` on `main` today; the stories ahead of this one add the rest.

## 3. In scope

**Backend (`internal/account`; small additive changes in `internal/orders`; one migration)**

1. `POST /v1/account/sign-up` (`services.md` §3.18): creates a role `customer` account, hashes the password with argon2id, signs the customer in with the shared session, 201 `{name, email, role}`; 422 naming each field; 409 for an email held by any account in any case; 429 after 10 attempts an hour from one address (`sign_up_address` counter).
2. `GET /v1/account` and `PUT /v1/account` (§3.19): the session's own name, email, phone, address, city; a partial change of name, phone, address and city; `email`, `role`, `password` refused with 422. Neither takes an id.
3. `GET /v1/account/orders` (newest first, ten at a time) and `GET /v1/account/orders/{id}` (§3.20): public view only (`id`, `number`, `placed_at`, `status`, `total`; the one-order read adds its pairs); never phone, email, address or note.
4. `POST /v1/account/orders` (§3.21): link a guest order to the signed-in account with its number and phone, matched exactly as the lookup does and counted on the lookup's `order_lookup_*` counters.
5. `POST /v1/orders` links the new order to the caller's account when the session's role is `customer`, and to none otherwise (a staff or admin session, no session, a session that ended since the page opened). `GET /v1/orders/{id}` gains the third way in (`services.md` §3.11): the session of the customer the order is linked to reads it without a token.
6. The migration for `orders.account_id` (nullable, FK to `users`, `ON DELETE SET NULL`) and its index `idx_orders_account_id_placed_at` (own file, `CONCURRENTLY`), plus any of `users.phone`, `users.address`, `users.city` that the sign-in migration did not create (TL's technical design settles which).

**Frontend**

7. Three new routes, each one additive `<Route>` line inside the customer shell in `src/App.tsx`: `/account/sign-up`, `/account/sign-in`, `/account`. Pages in `src/pages/` (`AccountSignUp.tsx`, `AccountSignIn.tsx`, `Account.tsx`), parts in `src/components/` split so no file passes the 300-line lint gate (for example the orders block, the saved-details block, the add-an-order block).
8. The story's API client `src/lib/customer-accounts-sign-up-sign-in-my-orders.ts`, on top of the one `src/lib/api.ts` (`credentials: "include"`). Anything this story adds to `src/lib/session.ts` is **additive** (for example a call that asks `/v1/auth/me` again after sign-up); `useSession`, `signIn`, `signOut`, `useRole` keep their names and meanings.
9. The header's "Account" entry in `SiteHeader.tsx` (bar from 1024px, menu below it), reading only `GET /v1/auth/me`.
10. `/checkout` fills its four contact fields from `GET /v1/account` for a signed-in customer. The edit to `pages/Checkout.tsx` is limited to the starting values of those fields.
11. For the UI stage only: a mock `src/lib/mock/customer-accounts-sign-up-sign-in-my-orders.ts` standing in for the `/v1/account/*` endpoints (a sample customer, an orders list of 0, 2 and 12, the refusals, a slow answer and an unreachable answer on demand). It must not store a password and must not read or write `localStorage` or `sessionStorage`; the backend PR deletes it.

## 4. Out of scope (and where it goes)

- **The shared sign-in, sign-out and `/v1/auth/me` endpoints, the cookie, the roles, the `users` and `sessions` tables, the sign-in limits and the `/admin` gate** — "Dealer sign-in and protected admin area". This story proves the customer's path through them (SC-5, SC-22, SC-23, SC-39) and builds nothing of them.
- **Placing an order, the Order page, `/track` and `POST /v1/order-lookups`** — "Checkout and placing an order", "Order page and Pay now", "Track an order by number and phone". They stay as they are for a guest; this story adds only the account link (item 5) and a page that links to the Order page. `Order.tsx` is not edited here.
- **Staff accounts** (create, switch off) — "Staff accounts managed by the owner". A staff row used in a test is inserted directly, as the sign-in story does.
- **Forgot password, change password, change email, delete account, sign in with another service, email verification** (ACC out of scope; SRS open question 5): no screen, no endpoint. See the accepted risks in the reply.
- **Email or text messages of any kind** — none is sent: no welcome message, no order confirmation, no reset link.
- **Unlinking an order, moving it to another account, cancelling or returning an order from My account, several saved addresses, wish lists** — not in the SRS and no screen in the design.
- **Going back to the page the visitor came from after signing in or up** — sign-up, sign-in and the team's redirect always land on `/account` or `/admin`, as the SRS says.
- **A list of customers or a customer's name on the dealer's order page** — the `admin` module; this story gives the dealer nothing new.
- **Owner-editable wording in the Studio.** SRS open question 4 asks for the new copy in the site's content, but `overview.md` §5 freezes `src/content.json` for stories (parallel stories would collide in it). The words in this story live in its components; the existing words (the Account link's neighbours, "Keep shopping", "We can't find that order.") keep coming from `content.json` untouched. A later chore can move them.
- **A CSRF token** — `SameSite=Lax`, JSON-only bodies and read-only `GET`s are the defence (`services.md` §2.2), as in the sign-in story.

## 5. UI scope

Screens and states, with what the approved design supplies and what this story adds:

| Screen | Built from | States this story draws |
|---|---|---|
| Sign up `/account/sign-up` | Customer shell (header, footer); display heading; text field + label (§2.1.16); filled pill (§2.1.6) for "Create account"; hairlines | default; field errors; email taken; creating; cannot reach the server; too many tries |
| Sign in `/account/sign-in` | Same parts | default; signing in; wrong email or password; cannot reach the server; too many tries |
| My account `/account` | Same parts: three blocks on hairlines — My orders (rows on hairlines like the order page's pairs), Saved delivery details (the checkout's four fields), Add an order (two fields) | loading orders; orders; no orders; show more; could not load + "Try again"; saving; saved; field errors; could not save; adding; added; order not found; too many tries; could not add |
| Header "Account" entry | SiteHeader link style (`text-sm`, underline on hover, `aria-current` on its own address); the menu's link style below 1024px | loading (nothing drawn); signed out ("Sign in"); signed in (name) |
| Checkout `/checkout` | Existing page | the four fields filled from the saved details |

Decisions made in this story (the SRS and the design system are silent or differ):

1. **Error placement.** The SRS says the sentence shows "beside the field"; the design system draws field errors as 12px `text-danger` lines with an alert icon and `role="alert"`. Each sentence sits directly **under its field** in that style.
2. **One filled pill per view** (design system §2.1.6): "Create account", "Sign in" and "Save details" are the filled pill of their view. "Add order", "Show more", "Try again", "Sign out" use the outline-pill classes of §2.1.5 as buttons (the header's search "Go" already does).
3. **A team session on `/account`** goes to `/admin`, like a team member signing in on `/account/sign-in` (the SRS says only that team sessions get 403 from the account endpoints).
4. **Copy** is exactly the SRS's words. Where this story needs a sentence the SRS does not give: blank sign-in fields use the dealer sign-in's "An email address is needed" and "A password is needed"; a saved-details refusal shows the shop's sentence for the field, and the client's own are "Fill in your name", "Check the phone number", "Use at most 200 characters" (address) and "Use at most 100 characters" (city).
5. **The header's name** is cut with an ellipsis in a width that never pushes the search and bag buttons off the bar.
6. **Tokens only** (`overview.md` §5): no raw colour, no arbitrary-value class, no CSS module; form buttons at least 44px high.

## 6. Acceptance criteria

Tags: **BE** = proved against the API (Go tests with `TEST_DATABASE_URL`, and HTTP calls to the running story); **UI** = proved in the browser and judged at the UI review on the mock; **UI+BE** = proved in the browser against the running story. Where a test needs time to pass (15 minutes, an hour) it moves the stored window or an injected clock and never sleeps. "The customer" in the examples is `Lan Nguyen`, `lan@example.com`, phone `0912 345 678`.

### Backend

- **SC-1 [ACC-001 AC-2, AC-3, item 3, Data touched; ACC-008 item 4, AC-6]** (BE): `POST /v1/account/sign-up` with name `Lan Nguyen`, email `lan@example.com`, phone `0912 345 678` and a 10-character password answers 201 `{"name":"Lan Nguyen","email":"lan@example.com","role":"customer"}` with the `session` cookie of the sign-in story (`HttpOnly`, `SameSite=Lax`, `Path=/`, 7 days); `GET /v1/auth/me` with that cookie says role `customer`. The email is stored in lower case. The role is always `customer`: a body carrying `role` answers 400 and makes no account. `users.password_hash` is an argon2id string that is not the password, and two accounts given the same password hold different hashes. No response and no log line holds the password or the hash.
- **SC-2 [ACC-001 AC-4, AC-5, item 4, Conflict row]** (BE): A sign-up with an email that belongs to a customer, to a staff account and to the admin account — each also typed in another case (`LAN@Example.com`) — answers 409 `conflict` with `errors.email` set and creates no row. Two sign-ups with one email at the same moment: one answers 201, the other 409, and one row exists.
- **SC-3 [ACC-001 AC-7, AC-8, item 2, Boundary row]** (BE): A sign-up answers 422 `invalid` with `errors` naming every field at fault, makes no account and sets no cookie, for: a missing or empty `name`, `email`, `phone` or `password` (a body with all four empty names all four); a name of 101 characters (100 is accepted); an email with no `@`, with no dot in its domain, with a space, or over 200 characters; a phone `0912 34` (`+84 912 345 678` is accepted; 21 characters are refused); a password of 7 characters (8 and 100 are accepted, 101 is refused). A password with spaces at its ends is stored as typed. A body that is not JSON-typed answers 415; an undefined field or a wrong type answers 400.
- **SC-4 [ACC-001 AC-11, item 6]** (BE): Ten sign-up attempts from one address within an hour are answered normally; the eleventh answers 429 `rate_limited` with `Retry-After` in whole seconds (at most 3600) and creates no account, even with valid details and a new email. Every attempt up to the limit counts, whether it was accepted or refused for a field or a taken email; a call refused with 429 does not extend the lock. The address is the right-most `X-Forwarded-For` entry. When the hour has passed, sign-up works again. Sign-up attempts do not count toward the sign-in limit, and sign-in failures do not count toward the sign-up limit.
- **SC-5 [ACC-002 AC-3, AC-4, AC-6, items 2, 4, Boundary row]** (BE): The customer signs in through the shared `POST /v1/auth/sign-in` (this story adds no sign-in route): the right credentials answer 200 `{"email","name","role":"customer"}`, and the email typed in another case reaches the same account. A wrong password and an unknown email answer the same status and body (401 `invalid_credentials`, "Wrong email or password."). After five wrong tries for one email, a sixth with the right password answers 429 with `Retry-After` (at most 900). A staff account and the admin account sign in through the same route and `GET /v1/auth/me` says `staff` and `admin`.
- **SC-6 [ACC-004 AC-1, AC-8, AC-9, item 2; ACC-008 AC-5, AC-6]** (BE): `GET /v1/account` for the customer just signed up answers 200 `{"name":"Lan Nguyen","email":"lan@example.com","phone":"0912 345 678","address":"","city":""}` and nothing else: no password, hash, role or id. It answers 401 with no session and 403 for a staff session and for the admin. The route takes no id and ignores `?id=`, `?account=` and `?user=`.
- **SC-7 [ACC-004 AC-2, AC-3, AC-4, AC-5, item 2]** (BE): `PUT /v1/account` with `{"address":"12 Example Street, Ward 1","city":"Da Nang"}` answers 200 with the same shape as `GET` and a later `GET` returns them; a body carrying only `phone` changes only the phone; a field left out is unchanged; `address` or `city` sent as `""` clears it. A body carrying `email`, `role` or `password` answers 422 naming that field and changes nothing, even for other fields in the same body. A phone of 8 digits names `phone`; an address of 201 characters names `address` (200 is accepted); a city of 101 names `city` (100 is accepted); a name of 101 or empty names `name`. Any other undefined field answers 400. 401 with no session, 403 for a team session.
- **SC-8 [ACC-003 AC-1, AC-3, AC-8, item 3; ACC-008 item 4]** (BE): With twelve orders linked to the customer, `GET /v1/account/orders` answers 200 `{"items","total":12,"page":1,"limit":10}` with ten items, newest first (`placed_at` descending, then the order's own sequence); `?page=2` answers the other two. Each item holds exactly `id`, `number`, `placed_at`, `status`, `total` and never `phone`, `email`, `address`, `note`, `customer` or `city`. A bad `page` or `limit` answers 400 naming it; another query parameter is ignored. 401 with no session, 403 for a team session. A customer with no order gets `"items":[]`, `"total":0`.
- **SC-9 [ACC-003 AC-6, AC-9, item 3; ACC-008 AC-3, item 3]** (BE): `GET /v1/account/orders/<id>` for one of the customer's own orders answers 200 with its public view and its pairs (no `phone`, `email`, `address`, `note`). For the order of another customer, a guest order (linked to none) and an id that does not exist it answers 404 `not_found` with **one and the same body**. 401 with no session, 403 for a team session.
- **SC-10 [ACC-003 item 4; ACC-008 AC-3, item 3; ORD-008 AC-11, AC-12]** (BE): `GET /v1/orders/<id>` with no `X-Order-Token` for an order linked to customer A: A's session answers 200 with the public view and without `phone`, `email`, `address`, `note`; customer B's session, no session, and an id that does not exist all answer 404 with one and the same body. With the order's own token the read still answers 200 as before.
- **SC-11 [ACC-005 AC-2, AC-3, AC-5, AC-7, items 2, 3, 5]** (BE): `POST /v1/orders` with the customer's session links the order to that account whatever name, phone and address were typed: the stored order holds the **typed** details and `GET /v1/account` is unchanged. The order is in `GET /v1/account/orders` at once and in the list of a second session of the same customer (a second sign-in); a second customer's list does not hold it. An order placed by a staff session and by the admin session is linked to none.
- **SC-12 [ACC-005 AC-4, AC-6, items 3, 4; ACC-006 item 6]** (BE): `POST /v1/orders` with no cookie, and with the cookie of a session that has ended (signed out, or expired) answers 201 with the order and its token and links it to none; the order is in no account's list; tracking by number and phone and the token still open it. The placing is never refused because the session ended.
- **SC-13 [ACC-006 AC-1, AC-5, AC-6, items 2, 5, 6]** (BE): With guest order `DOI-24818` placed for phone `0912 345 678`, the customer's `POST /v1/account/orders` with number `doi 24818` and phone `+84912345678` answers 200 `{"id","number","placed_at","status","total"}`; the order is then in `GET /v1/account/orders`. Adding it again answers 200 and the list still holds one row. The number is also accepted as `24818`, `DOI-24818` and `doi-24818`, and the phone as `0912345678` and `84 912 345 678`. The guest browser's token still reads the order and `POST /v1/order-lookups` with the same pair still finds it.
- **SC-14 [ACC-006 AC-2, AC-3, AC-8, item 3, Not found row]** (BE): A wrong phone, an unknown number, an empty phone, a phone that matches only in part, and an order already linked to **another customer's** account all answer 404 `not_found` with one and the same body; none of them links anything; an undefined field or a wrong type answers 400.
- **SC-15 [ACC-006 AC-4, AC-7, item 4, item 7; ORD-022]** (BE): After five failures for one order number, or from one address, within 15 minutes — counted on the lookup's own `order_lookup_*` counters, so failures from `POST /v1/account/orders` and from `POST /v1/order-lookups` add up — a sixth try with the right pair answers 429 with `Retry-After` (at most 900) and links nothing; a success neither counts nor clears. `POST /v1/account/orders` answers 401 with no session and 403 for a team session.
- **SC-16 [ACC-008 AC-1, AC-4, AC-5, items 1, 2, 4]** (BE): With the customer's session, `GET /v1/orders`, `POST /v1/products`, `GET /v1/admin/overview` and `GET /v1/admin/staff` each answer 403 (a route that is not built when this story is verified is checked on a route registered by the test behind the same `auth.Require` guard, as the sign-in story's SC-18 does). A second customer who knows the number of customer A's order but not its phone gets the generic 404 from `POST /v1/order-lookups`. The account routes are `/v1/account/sign-up`, `/v1/account`, `/v1/account/orders` and `/v1/account/orders/{id}` (an **order** id): none takes an account id and none returns another customer's data.
- **SC-17 [ACC-003 Performance NFR; `erd.md` §8 migrations 11–12]** (BE): The migration adds nullable `orders.account_id` (foreign key to `users(id)`, `ON DELETE SET NULL`) and, in a file of its own, `idx_orders_account_id_placed_at` created `CONCURRENTLY IF NOT EXISTS`, plus any `users` column this story needs that the sign-in migration did not make. It applies on boot on a database that already holds orders (every existing order has `account_id` NULL), a second boot changes nothing, and deleting a customer row leaves their orders with `account_id` NULL. With 100 orders linked to one customer, `GET /v1/account/orders` answers within 300 ms at p95 over 100 sequential requests. `go build`, `go vet` and `go test ./...` pass with no database (database tests skip).
