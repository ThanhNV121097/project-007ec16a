# Story — Manage shoes, lines, categories, reviews and orders

Module: `admin` · Plan item 10 · Requirements narrowed: ADMIN-010, ADMIN-011, ADMIN-012, ADMIN-013, ADMIN-014, ADMIN-015 and the write half of ADMIN-004 (`docs/admin/SRS.md` §4.1, §4.2).
**The order half of this item is split out.** ADMIN-016 (moving an order along, courier details, the paid state) and the order parts of ADMIN-004, ADMIN-011 and ADMIN-014 are now the plan item **Dealer handles orders: status, courier, payment** (section 4). Together the two came to about 60 acceptance criteria, and a single verification round cannot carry that; the owner's two decisions on this item (online payment only; staff post the shoes and the quantities) are recorded where they apply.
Design system: `design/design-system.md` §2.2 (management area, scope `[data-dashboard]`) · Architecture: `docs/architecture/overview.md`; `erd.md` (`products`, `product_sizes`, view `product_stock`, `reviews`, `shop_terms`, `uploads`); `services.md` (§2.4, §2.5, §2.8, §3.22 writes, §3.23 one size's count, §3.24 uploads). The contracts there are the starting point; TL's design step for this story may extend them (section 8).

## 1. User story

As **the shop's team** — the admin, and the staff the admin adds; both may do everything below — I want to post shoes with their price, pictures and the number of pairs in each size, keep the lines, categories and reviews, and correct one size's quantity on its own, so that the shop shows real shoes that are really on the shelf, and everything I save is still there after a reload.

Other actors this story touches:

- **Visitor** and **customer** — see only shoes that are on sale and reviews that are shown, with a rating that follows those reviews, and sizes that follow the counts; their endpoints for writing answer 401 (visitor) and 403 (customer session).
- **Owner** — decided that staff and admins post the products and adjust the quantities by hand: there is no supplier feed and no import. This story is how the shop gets its real catalogue.

## 2. What exists today

- `src/components/admin/store.ts` (`saveRow`, `deleteRow`, `newId`) writes into the browser's memory through `setCollection`; `ListPage` and `RecordPage` call it straight away, close the dialog at once and know no saving, error or refusal state. A reload brings back what the database holds.
- The picture control in `Form.tsx` reads the chosen file with `FileReader` and keeps it on the record as `data:` text.
- The shoe form's Stock tab holds four hand-typed fields — "Sizes (EU)", "Last pairs in", "Sold out in", "Pairs in stock" — with no count per size.
- On the server `internal/data` and `internal/catalogue` only read; they serve every shoe (drafts included) and every review (hidden included) to everyone. There is no write route and no upload. `auth.Require` and `internal/admin` (staff accounts only) are on `main`; `src/lib/api.ts` has `get`, `post`, `put` and `delete`.
- `product_sizes`, the view `product_stock`, `internal/stock` and `internal/orders` come from the **Checkout** story (plan item 5, PR #50) and are not on `main` yet. Today a shoe's sizes and stock are columns of `products`; this story does not touch that model and must not keep a second copy of stock.

## 3. In scope

**Backend (Go, PostgreSQL) — dealer session only, 401 with none and 403 for a customer**

1. `POST /v1/products`, `/v1/lines`, `/v1/categories`, `/v1/reviews`; `PUT /v1/<collection>/{id}` (only the fields sent change); `DELETE /v1/<collection>/{id}` — `services.md` §3.22. Checked on the server against `schema.json`: required fields, types, closed sets (a shoe's status and main colour; a review's fit and status), references (a shoe's line and category, a review's shoe), price ≥ 0, a review's stars 1–5, text ≤ 200 characters and long text ≤ 5,000. A refusal is 422 `{"errors":{"<field>":"<sentence>"}}` naming every field at fault, with nothing saved. A field the collection does not have, or a derived one (`rating`, `review_count`, `stock`, `sizes_low`, `sizes_out`), is refused the same way.
2. The shop makes the id: a shoe, line or category gets the slug of its name (`-2`, `-3` while taken); a review gets `r-` and eight hex characters. `featured` is exclusive: saving a shoe with "Opens the home page" on turns it off on every other shoe. A save is one transaction; two saves of one record: the later wins.
3. **Visitor view and dealer view on reads** (`GET /v1/products`, `/v1/products/{id}`, `/v1/reviews`, `/v1/reviews/{id}`, search and the size filter, `GET /v1/product-filters`): a visitor or a customer gets only shoes with status live and reviews with status live — a draft shoe by id is 404, `?status=draft` is `total: 0`; the dealer gets everything, and a shoe carries `pairs`, `price_listed` and `_source` for the dealer only.
4. **Ratings follow the shown reviews.** A shoe's `rating` (average of its live reviews' stars, one decimal) and `review_count` are recomputed in the same transaction on every review event (create; change of stars, status or shoe; delete) and when the shoe is saved; the shop's `rating` and `review_count` (`shop_terms`) follow the live reviews of live shoes and are recomputed on the same events and when a shoe goes live or to draft.
5. **Deleting safely:** a shoe any order refers to — 409 "Orders refer to this shoe. Set it to draft instead."; a shoe no order refers to goes with its reviews and its counts; a line or category some shoe uses — 409 "<n> shoes use this. Move them first."; a review is always deletable.
6. **Pictures:** `POST /v1/uploads` (§3.24) — JPEG, PNG or WebP judged by content, at most 5 MB (5,242,880 bytes), stored under a name the shop makes (migration `uploads`), `201 {"url": "/api/v1/uploads/<name>"}`; 415 "Choose a JPEG, PNG or WebP picture."; 413 "That picture is too large (5 MB at most)."; served to everyone by `GET /v1/uploads/{name}`.
7. **The pairs in each size** (ADMIN-015): a shoe's `pairs` (size → whole number ≥ 0) is written with the shoe on `POST` / `PUT`, and one size's count alone by `PATCH /v1/products/{id}/sizes` with the count the person last saw (§3.23). The counts live **only** in `product_sizes`, the same rows an order takes its pairs from (`stock.Take`); a shoe's `sizes`, `sizes_low` (1 or 2 pairs), `sizes_out` (0 pairs) and `stock` (the sum) are derived from them through `product_stock` on every read and are never typed. A count moved by an order since the person looked is refused with 409; nothing is overwritten silently and no count goes below 0.
8. A save or delete answers within 1 s at p95.

**Frontend (Vite + React + Tailwind tokens)**

9. `src/components/admin/store.ts` — `saveRow`, `deleteRow`, `newId` keep their names and now call the shop through `src/lib/api.ts` (`credentials: "include"`); they return promises and report a refusal with its field errors, "session ended", "unreachable", or the shop's sentence. `newId` stays exported for what the browser needs before the shop answers; no id made in the browser is ever sent as a record's id. `setCollection` is untouched (the Studio still uses it). The API client for these calls is `src/lib/manage-shoes-lines-categories-reviews-and-orders.ts` on top of `api.ts` (no second file with `api` in its name).
10. `ListPage.tsx` and `RecordPage.tsx` await them and show: the saving state ("Saving…", button disabled), the field errors beside their fields with the typed values kept, "We could not save. Please try again.", "Your session ended. Sign in again." with a link to `/admin/sign-in`, a delete refusal inside the open dialog; after a success the dialog closes (or "Saved." shows) and the list or record is **re-read from the shop**, never redrawn from what was typed. `AdminProducts`, `AdminProduct`, `AdminLines`, `AdminKinds`, `AdminReviews` keep their design and read the dealer view.
11. The picture control (`Form.tsx`) sends the file to `POST /v1/uploads` and keeps the returned address on the record; it shows it is working, makes Save wait, and shows a refusal beside the control.
12. The shoe form's Stock: for each EU size, a labelled whole-number count; "Last pairs in", "Sold out in" and "Pairs in stock" shown read-only and redrawn from the shop's answer; on the shoe's page each existing size's count saves by itself (on blur or Enter) with "Saving…", "Saved.", the 409 sentence, or "We could not save. Please try again.".
13. Tests: `src/test/admin.test.ts` pins the old in-memory store and is rewritten for the API-backed one; new tests beside it for the states above.

## 5. Decisions made here (the SRS leaves them open)

1. **A new shoe starts as "Draft".** The Add dialog's Status field preselects "Draft" and always sends the status (`erd.md` open question Q6), so a half-filled shoe is never public; staff choose "On sale" when it is ready. The tests choose "On sale" explicitly.
2. **A form save never silently overwrites a count.** Counts of existing sizes are saved on their own (SC-21, SC-23) and a whole-form save that carries counts is refused with 409 for any size an order moved since the page was read (SC-23); a form in which no count was touched carries none (SC-24). `services.md` §3.22 makes `pairs` authoritative as drafted — TL's design step adds the guard (section 8).
3. **Sizes are typed as people write them** (`38`, `36 2/3`, `35 1/2`, `35.5`), whitespace collapsed, at most 20 characters; the shop must be able to read one as a number or the save is 422 naming it.
4. **The id is the shop's.** `newId` is never sent; after "Add" the list shows the id the shop made.
5. **The new words** ("Saving…", "We could not save. Please try again.", "Your session ended. Sign in again.", "We could not upload that picture. Please try again.", the stock-row messages) live in the admin components beside the existing "Add", "Cancel", "Save changes" and "Nothing here yet"; `src/content.json` is not touched (the dealer sign-in story's precedent, and nothing else collides in it). A later chore can move them if the owner wants to edit them in the Studio.
6. **Seeded placeholders.** The shoes, reviews and ratings that came with the first design are ordinary records: staff may set them to draft at any time and delete them when no order refers to them. Their ratings and review counts, and the shop's 4.8 from 181 reviews, stay as seeded until a review event recalculates them (ADMIN-013 item 4); no criterion pins a sample shoe's stock total (`erd.md` Q1).
7. **Private order fields on the dealer's reads** are not widened here (that is the order item), so after this story `GET /v1/orders` is as item 9 left it.

## 7. Acceptance criteria

Thirty-nine criteria. The SRS gives these requirements about 85 criteria of their own; each is traced below, several of them to one criterion, and the order ones are in the split-out item (section 4). Every criterion is checked against the running story: pages in a browser, endpoints with real sessions — an admin, a staff account, a customer row inserted into `users` for the test, and no session. "The dealer view" and "the visitor view" are `services.md` §2.4's two views of a shoe or a review.

Tags: **BE** = proved against the API (Go tests with `TEST_DATABASE_URL`, and HTTP calls to the running story); **UI** = proved in the browser, judged at the UI review on the mock; **UI+BE** = proved in the browser against the running story.

### 7.1 Who may write

- **SC-1 [ADMIN-004 AC-1, AC-2, AC-3, AC-4, AC-9; ADMIN-015 AC-12]** (BE): With no session, and with a forged or expired `session` cookie, each of these answers 401 with the error envelope and no data, and nothing is created, changed or deleted: `POST`, `PUT` and `DELETE` on products, lines, categories and reviews; `POST /v1/uploads`; `PATCH /v1/products/<id>/sizes`. `GET /v1/products` with no session is still 200.
- **SC-2 [ADMIN-004 AC-7; ADMIN-015 AC-12]** (BE): The same calls with a signed-in customer session answer 403 with the error envelope and change nothing; a customer who reads `GET /v1/products` is served as a visitor.
- **SC-3 [ADMIN-004 AC-10; ADMIN-015 AC-13; ADMIN-010 item 1]** (BE): An admin session and a staff session each create a shoe (201), change it (200), change one size's count (200), upload a picture (201) and delete a shoe (204), with the same answers. For a dealer, `POST`, `PUT` and `DELETE` on delivery, payments, shop, sizes and faqs answer 404 (no write route exists); their `GET`s are unchanged.

### 7.2 Saving records

- **SC-4 [ADMIN-010 AC-1, AC-2, AC-3; items 5, 9]** (BE): `POST /v1/products` with a name, price 3290000, line `samba`, category `women` and status `live` answers 201 with `Location: /v1/products/<id>` and the record in the dealer view; the id is the slug of the name (lower case, accents removed, `đ` as `d`, hyphens), so "Samba OG New Colour" is `samba-og-new-colour`; a second shoe with the same name gets `-2`, a third `-3`. A new line or category gets its name's slug the same way, and a new review `r-` followed by eight hex characters. The very next `GET /v1/products/<id>` by a visitor answers 200 with the saved values.
- **SC-5 [ADMIN-010 AC-4, AC-10, AC-11; item 3]** (BE): Each of these answers 422 `{"errors":{…}}` naming the field, and saves nothing: a shoe without a name, a shoe without a price, a line or category without a name, a review without a shoe or a customer name; two faults at once (no name and price −1) name both; a field the collection does not have (`colour_code`) or a derived one (`rating`, `review_count`, `stock`, `sizes_low`, `sizes_out`) names that field. A body that is not a JSON object answers 400. Every sentence is in English and short enough to sit beside its field.
- **SC-6 [ADMIN-010 AC-5, AC-6, AC-7, AC-8, AC-9; item 2]** (BE): A price of −1 answers 422 naming `price` ("Price cannot be below 0"); `"abc"` and 2.5 answer 422 naming `price`; 0 is accepted (201). A status of `archived` names `status`; a main colour outside the closed list names `colour`; a line, a category or a review's shoe that does not exist names `line`, `category` or `product`; a `released` that is not a `YYYY-MM-DD` date names `released` (`erd.md` Q2); `""` unsets a shoe's line or category.
- **SC-7 [ADMIN-010 AC-12, AC-13; item 2]** (BE): A review's stars of 6, 0 and 2.5 each answer 422 naming `rating`; 1, 5 and an empty value are accepted. A name of exactly 200 characters is accepted and one of 201 is refused naming the limit; a description of 5,000 characters is accepted and 5,001 is refused. Limits count characters, so a Vietnamese letter with marks counts once.
- **SC-8 [ADMIN-010 AC-14, AC-15, AC-18; item 4; ADMIN-014 "Not found"]** (BE): `PUT /v1/products/<id>` carrying only `price` changes the price and nothing else — every other field, the id, the counts and the derived lists stay as they were — and the next visitor read shows the new price at once. A different `id` in the body is ignored. `PUT` and `DELETE` of an id that does not exist answer 404 on each of the four collections; a required field sent as `null` answers 422.
- **SC-9 [ADMIN-010 AC-19; item 7]** (BE): Two `PUT`s of one shoe's price sent at the same moment both answer 200, and the stored price is that of the later one; the record is whole, with no mix of the two saves.
- **SC-10 [ADMIN-010 AC-16; item 6]** (BE): Shoe A opens the home page; saving shoe B with "Opens the home page" on answers 200, after which A reads `featured: false` and B `true`, and the home page shows B. Two saves that both turn it on at the same moment leave exactly one shoe with it.

### 7.3 What visitors see, and the ratings

- **SC-11 [ADMIN-013 AC-1, AC-2, AC-3, AC-4; ADMIN-010 AC-17; item 1]** (BE): A draft shoe is absent from a visitor's `GET /v1/products`, from the results of a `q` search and of the `size` filter, and its colours and sizes do not appear in `GET /v1/product-filters`; `GET /v1/products/<id>` answers 404 with the body of an id that does not exist; `GET /v1/products?status=draft` has `total: 0`. A hidden review is absent from `GET /v1/reviews` and `GET /v1/reviews?product=<id>`, and is 404 by id. The same calls with an admin or staff session return them (200), and the shoe then carries `pairs`, `price_listed` and `_source`; a customer session reads as a visitor.
- **SC-12 [ADMIN-013 AC-5, AC-6, AC-7, AC-8; item 2]** (BE): A shoe with live reviews of 5 and 4 stars reads `rating` 4.5 and `review_count` 2; a third live review of 4 makes them 4.3 and 3 (one decimal). Hiding a review, changing its stars, moving it to another shoe and deleting it each recompute both figures of every shoe affected in the same transaction as the review's own save; deleting the last live review gives 0 and 0, and the shoe page then shows no rating line.
- **SC-13 [ADMIN-013 AC-9, AC-10; items 3, 4]** (BE): With two live shoes that have live reviews, `GET /v1/shop` reads `rating` and `review_count` equal to the average and the number of all those reviews. Setting one shoe to draft (or deleting it) takes its reviews out of the figures and putting it back on sale puts them in; with no live review anywhere both are 0. The figures that came with the first design (the shop's 4.8 from 181 reviews, each shoe's seeded rating) are still what is read until a review event recalculates them.

### 7.4 Deleting safely

- **SC-14 [ADMIN-014 AC-1, AC-2; item 1; ADMIN-015 AC-15]** (BE): `DELETE` of a shoe an order refers to — a placeholder shoe the sample orders refer to included — answers 409 "Orders refer to this shoe. Set it to draft instead."; the shoe, its reviews, its counts and the order are untouched. Saving the same shoe as `draft` answers 200: it leaves the visitor's reads, the dealer still reads it, and the order still shows it.
- **SC-15 [ADMIN-014 AC-3; item 2; ADMIN-015 AC-15]** (BE): `DELETE` of a shoe no order refers to — a placeholder included — that has two reviews and counts for three sizes answers 204; the shoe, its two reviews and its three counts are gone (each `GET` is 404), the shop's rating and review count are recomputed, and a second `DELETE` is 404.
- **SC-16 [ADMIN-014 AC-4, AC-5, AC-6, AC-9; items 3, 4]** (BE): A line used by three shoes answers `DELETE` with 409 "3 shoes use this. Move them first."; a category in use answers 409 the same way; once the shoes are moved each answers 204, and a line or category no shoe uses answers 204. A review answers 204 whatever its status.

### 7.5 Pictures

- **SC-17 [ADMIN-012 AC-1, AC-2; items 1, 2]** (BE): A dealer's `POST /v1/uploads` of a 1 MB JPEG, then a 1 MB PNG and a 1 MB WebP, answers 201 `{"url":"/api/v1/uploads/<32 hex>.<jpg|png|webp>"}`; `GET /v1/uploads/<name>` as a visitor returns the same bytes with the right `Content-Type`, a long cache header and `nosniff`.
- **SC-18 [ADMIN-012 AC-3, AC-4; item 3]** (BE): A GIF answers 415 "Choose a JPEG, PNG or WebP picture."; a text file renamed `x.jpg` answers 415 (the content decides, not the name or the declared type) and a real PNG named `x.txt` is accepted; an empty file answers 422; a request that is not multipart, has no `file` part or carries a second part answers 400. Nothing is stored for a refusal.
- **SC-19 [ADMIN-012 AC-5, AC-6; boundary]** (BE): A JPEG of exactly 5,242,880 bytes is accepted (201); one byte more answers 413 "That picture is too large (5 MB at most)." and stores nothing. A file named `../../x.png` is stored under the shop's own 32-hex name: the returned address holds no part of the sent name and nothing is written outside the pictures' store.

### 7.6 The pairs in each size

- **SC-20 [ADMIN-015 AC-1, AC-2, AC-7; items 1, 2, 6]** (BE): A staff member's `POST /v1/products` with a price, three pictures, status `live` and `pairs` `{"38":4,"39":2,"40":0}` answers 201; the dealer view carries `pairs`, `sizes` `["38","39","40"]`, `sizes_low` `["39"]`, `sizes_out` `["40"]` and `stock` 6, and the visitor view the same lists and total without `pairs`. `GET /v1/products?size=40` does not contain the shoe; `?size=38` and `?size=39` do (a size on its last pairs still counts). A shoe whose counts add up to 18 reads `stock` 18.
- **SC-21 [ADMIN-015 AC-3, AC-4, AC-5; item 4]** (BE): `PATCH /v1/products/<id>/sizes` with `{"size","pairs","seen_pairs"}` answers 200 with the size, its new count and the shoe's redrawn `sizes`, `sizes_low`, `sizes_out` and `stock`. Size 38 from 4 to 0 puts 38 in `sizes_out` and the shoe no longer matches `?size=38`; from 5 to 2 puts it in `sizes_low`; from 2 to 3 takes it out of both lists. A size the shoe does not list answers 404; a missing or non-whole `pairs` or `seen_pairs` answers 422.
- **SC-22 [ADMIN-015 AC-8; item 3]** (BE): On `POST` and `PUT`, a count of −1, 2.5 or `"abc"` answers 422 naming `pairs.<size>`; a size added without a count answers 422 naming that size ("Enter the pairs in this size") whereas 0 is accepted; a size the shop cannot read as a number, a duplicate size, and a `sizes` list that disagrees with `pairs` each answer 422 naming it. Nothing changes in any of these cases.
- **SC-23 [ADMIN-015 AC-9; Concurrency]** (BE): Size 38 holds 4 and the page showed 4; an order then takes 2. `PATCH` with `pairs` 6 and `seen_pairs` 4 answers 409 "Pairs were just taken by an order. Check the number and save again." and the stored count stays 2. A whole-shoe `PUT` whose counts were read before the order answers 409 naming the size and stores nothing from that save. The same `PATCH` with `seen_pairs` 2 answers 200. An order and a count change arriving together never leave a count below 0 and never overwrite pairs an order took.
